# ZeroTrace — Future Scope

## 1. Vision

ZeroTrace is designed as a foundation for a comprehensive AI reliability and adversarial evaluation platform.

The current system provides the core experience for evaluating AI agents, analyzing reliability, viewing evaluation history, and generating reports.

Future versions can extend this foundation into a continuously operating AI reliability and security platform.

---

## 2. Real AI Model Integration

The next major step is to connect ZeroTrace with real AI models and autonomous AI agents.

Future integrations may include:

- Large Language Models
- Autonomous AI agents
- Tool-using AI systems
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems
- Custom enterprise AI models

This will allow ZeroTrace to evaluate real AI behavior instead of relying primarily on prototype evaluation workflows.

---

## 3. Advanced Adversarial Testing

TRASY can be extended with a larger and more dynamic collection of adversarial evaluation scenarios.

Future testing areas may include:

- Prompt injection
- Instruction conflicts
- Context manipulation
- Behavioral stress testing
- Tool-use failures
- Unexpected inputs
- Reliability degradation
- Adversarial reasoning scenarios

The goal will be to continuously discover weaknesses that may not appear during normal AI interactions.

---

## 4. Automated Test Generation

Future versions can introduce AI-assisted test generation.

Instead of relying only on predefined scenarios, TRASY could generate new evaluation cases based on:

- Previous evaluation results
- Detected weaknesses
- Agent capabilities
- Threat level
- Evaluation history
- Behavioral patterns

This would make evaluations more adaptive and difficult for AI agents to predict.

---
